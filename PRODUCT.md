# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Content creators, coaches, e-commerce brands, personal brands and small businesses making short-form video (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts). Range from zero followers to ~60K. Use it on desktop and phone, day and evening. (Source: .impeccable.md, confirmed by the founder earlier.)

## Product Purpose
The Content Labs studies a creator's own videos (and competitors') and tells them what to post next and why: video-by-video reports (hook, beat map, scorecard), a strategy, a 30-day calendar, and the Chemist, an AI strategist that writes scripts from the creator's own data. Success: a creator knows exactly what their next three videos are and believes it because the numbers are theirs.

## Positioning
Analysis of the creator's own performance, not generic AI writing. Free plan tracks 10 videos per platform weekly and fully analyzes 3; paid plans ($19/$39/$99, first month $5) add competitors, strategy, calendar and unlimited analysis.

## Operating Context
Marketing site thecontentlabs.app (Next.js, repo thecontentlabs-lp). App at /dashboard (Vite + React, this repo), Supabase back end, weekly emails (lab note for paid, numbers for free).

## Brand Commitments
Name: The Content Labs; the AI is "the Chemist". Coral #f4632a accent, cream light theme, deep navy dark theme. Clash Display headings, DM Sans body. Personality: energizing, guiding, confident. References: Raycast, Notion, Linear. Anti-references: HubSpot, Mixpanel, generic ChatGPT wrappers.

## Evidence on Hand
21,000+ videos analyzed (cross-user count). No customer testimonials are approved for use; do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Speed is a feature.
2. Guide, don't overwhelm: show what matters now, reveal depth on demand.
3. Warm precision: human feel, meticulous spacing.
4. The lab earns its name only where it helps the user.
5. Personality through restraint: one bold choice per screen.

_Inferred, not interviewed: the founder asked for autonomous execution; facts above come from .impeccable.md and the founder's own messages._
